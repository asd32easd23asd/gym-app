# Gym Planner app icon

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool, then mechanically downscaled to the 1024 × 1024 opaque RGB PNG required by the app asset catalog. No visual composition edits were made after generation.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Final production iOS app icon, a single square 1024 x 1024 PNG image.
Primary request: Create a polished, distinctive icon for an offline gym training, strength-progress and supplement-tracking app called Gym Planner. No lettering or words in the image.
Scene/backdrop: Entire square filled edge-to-edge with near-black graphite #111214. Flat opaque full-bleed background.
Subject: One large, bold, centered orange geometric athletic emblem combining a rising chevron / upward strength progression with the silhouette of a compact barbell. Make it feel like a memorable purpose-designed gym app symbol rather than a stock dumbbell illustration. Keep the emblem exceptionally simple and optically balanced with thick robust forms and a clear silhouette visible at 60 pixels.
Style/medium: Refined modern flat vector-like logo rendered as crisp high-resolution raster. Precise geometric edges, a small amount of tasteful dimensional shading in the orange is permissible but no distracting texture. Premium sports-performance identity.
Composition/framing: Single emblem centered, about 60 percent of square width, generous consistent breathing room. No separate rounded-square tile: the entire image itself is the app icon and iOS will apply its own corner mask.
Color palette: Vibrant warm orange #ff7043 for emblem, graphite #111214 background, optional subtle darker orange surfaces only.
Constraints: Exact square canvas, fully opaque, no transparency, no outer rounded corners, no border or framing stroke, no additional glyphs, no letters, no text, no numbers, no watermark, no equipment scene, no background pattern, no mockup, no green, no flower or starburst motif.
